UltronClips Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

UltronClips is a personal content workflow used by its account owner.

The service processes only the video files, captions, and post details the account owner provides or approves. It does not sell personal information or provide user data to third parties for advertising.

TikTok account access, if connected, is used only to upload approved content as drafts. The account owner reviews and manually publishes each post.

Access credentials are kept private and are not displayed publicly.

For privacy questions, contact the account owner through this repository’s issue tracker.
